Connection

↓ Gap 0–44

Experience isn’t matching what you promise

The gap between your stated commitments and what stakeholders actually encounter day-to-day is where trust erodes fastest — quietly, before it shows in numbers.

Map the experience gap: where do touchpoints diverge from the promise? Fix the top two.

Connection is inconsistent across touchpoints

Some stakeholder groups experience you well; others less so. The risk is that loyalty is relationship-dependent rather than institutionally embedded.

Identify which touchpoints are strongest and replicate those behaviours systematically.

↑ Strength 72–100

Stakeholder relationships are a core strength

Your Connection score suggests people experience you as coherent and reliable. This is hard to build and easy to lose — it deserves to be measured and protected.

Quantify and communicate this strength: retention data, relationship depth, consistency metrics.

Confidence

↓ Gap 0–44

Your brand’s distinctive advantage isn’t visible

Good work is happening, but it isn’t being claimed. Stakeholders can’t verify claims, see progress, or point to evidence. This makes loyalty transactional.

Identify your three strongest proof points and make them visible — in every channel, consistently.

~ Watch 45–71

Evidence exists but isn’t structured for impact

↑ Strength 72–100

Your brand’s distinction is clear and credible
