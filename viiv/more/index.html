01 · The mission, measured against the experience

Listen is objective one.
Nobody has measured it.

From Positive Perspectives — ViiV's own study, and the strongest patient-listening programme in HIV. Three things are true at once:

80% of people living with HIV report high trust in their healthcare provider.Positive Perspectives 3 (interim) — ViiV's own study · n=698 · 16 countries · 2025
47.5% sometimes feel unheard by that same provider.Positive Perspectives 3 · 2025
39.7% didn’t make the decision about their own treatment jointly with their provider.Positive Perspectives 3 · 2025

Being left behind does not announce itself. And this is the relationship ViiV listens to most — the clinics, commissioners, communities and ViiV's own people are not measured like this at all. You can't close a gap you can't see.

02 · How TrustOS closes it

Three questions, asked of every relationship. One shared picture.

Turned into a shape, a band, and a ranked list of what to fix.

Clarity
Did the message that arrived match the message you sent?
Can they say back what ViiV stands for?
Connection
What did it feel like to be on the other side of you?
Not what you meant — what they experienced.
Confidence
Would they vouch for you when you're not in the room?
Evidence in place of assurance.

This is healthcare's shape, read from public signal: Fragile — trust present, not yet load-bearing, with Connection its weakest edge.

ViiV's own shape has never been measured. A baseline in six weeks, read again every quarter, each wave landing with the gaps ranked.

03 · What ViiV gets

Not a score. A map, a next move, and a lesson.

ViiV measures almost everything it does, and almost nothing about how it's experienced. Four jobs:

Spot the leaks
See which relationship is weakening — before the performance numbers say so.
Over 75% of HIV growth runs through relationships ViiV doesn't control — and trust erodes there while adherence and uptake still look fine.
Prove impact
Engagement, evidenced — to the board, and for the disclosure era.
GSK is in CSRD scope from FY2027. Measured outcomes satisfy what descriptions of activity can't.
Make the next move
The room aligned on what to fix first.
A ranking settles what moves first — so the conversation is about what to do, not whether to believe it.
Learn from every move
The question no ranking answers: are your own relationships getting stronger?
Rankings measure standing, not direction. This shows each relationship's direction of travel.
NPS reads one question. Brand tracking measures what's said. Employee surveys read one group. None locates which relationship is weakening, on which driver, or what to do about it.
