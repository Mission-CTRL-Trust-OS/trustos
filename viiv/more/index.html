How ViiV hears itself — the two-minute version

Making HIV history runs on trust.
Most of it is unmeasured.

No company listens to people living with HIV the way ViiV does — Positive Perspectives proves that. TrustOS extends that same discipline to every relationship the mission runs through. Concretely: a quarterly measure of how much the people ViiV depends on trust ViiV — scored, tracked, owned by ViiV, built by Mission CTRL.

01 · The promise, measured against the experience

High trust. Unheard.

From ViiV’s own study — the strongest patient-listening programme in HIV. Both things are true at once:

80% of people living with HIV report high trust in their healthcare provider.Positive Perspectives 3 (interim) — ViiV's own study · n=698 · 16 countries · 2025
47.5% sometimes feel unheard by that same provider.Positive Perspectives 3 · 2025
39.7% didn’t make the decision about their own treatment jointly with their provider.Positive Perspectives 3 · 2025

Feeling unheard is where engagement quietly leaks — adherence, retention, uptake of new options: the things making HIV history depends on. And this is the relationship ViiV listens to most. The clinics delivering long-acting care every eight weeks, the commissioners, the communities, ViiV’s own people — the mission now runs through them all, and none of them are measured like this. You can’t close a gap you can’t see.

02 · How TrustOS closes it

Three questions, asked of every relationship. One shared picture.

Every read asks the same three plain questions of each stakeholder group — and turns the answers into a score, a shape, and a ranked list of what to fix.

Clarity
Did the message that arrived match the message you sent?
Strong Clarity means stakeholders can say back what ViiV stands for, in their own words. The sector shows what weak looks like: 39% of healthcare workers across Europe and Central Asia still lack knowledge of U=U, and 42% of England's HIV diagnoses still arrive late.
ECDC/EACS survey, 2024 report · UKHSA, Oct 2025
Connection
What did it feel like to be on the other side of you?
Not what you meant — what they experienced. ViiV's own study is the sharpest picture anywhere of the gap: 80% report high trust; 47.5% sometimes feel unheard — the same people.
Positive Perspectives 3 (interim), 2025
Confidence
Would they vouch for you when you're not in the room?
Evidence in place of assurance — in a sector where claims are discounted by default: pharma ranks 24th of 25 industries in Gallup's ratings. Proof is the only currency that clears.
Gallup industry ratings, Aug 2025

TrustOS has already read twelve sectors from public signal — this is healthcare's shape, the sector ViiV lives in. It reads as Fragile: trust present, not yet load-bearing, with Connection — feeling heard — its weakest edge, as in 11 of the 12 sectors read.

ViiV's own shape has never been measured. The cycle that measures it is Sense → Measure → Act: a baseline in six weeks, read again every quarter, each wave landing with the gaps ranked and the actions behind them.

03 · What ViiV gets

Not a score. A map, a next move, and a lesson.

ViiV measures almost everything it does — trials, uptake, access, grants — and almost nothing about how it's experienced. Applied to ViiV, the instrument's four jobs:

Spot the leaks
See which relationship is weakening — before the performance numbers say so.
More than 75% of HIV growth now runs through relationships ViiV doesn't control — the clinics, commissioners and communities who deliver long-acting care every eight weeks. The read shows where trust is quietly eroding across those groups while adherence, retention and uptake still look fine.
HowEvery read is broken down by stakeholder group, driver and market. Not a level — a location: which relationship, which driver, where.
Prove impact
Engagement, evidenced — to the board, and for the disclosure era.
GSK is in CSRD scope from FY2027: how engagement with affected communities is organised becomes auditable disclosure. Measured outcomes satisfy what descriptions of activity can't — and show whether "here until you aren't" is landing the way it's meant.
HowScores, trends and evidence per relationship, inside an instrument ViiV owns. Live in six weeks; board-ready from the first read.
Make the next move
The room aligned on what to fix first.
Patient engagement, medical, access and comms each hold a piece of the same relationships. A measured ranking settles what moves first — so the conversation after a read is about what to do, not whether to believe the finding.
HowEach wave lands with the gaps ranked and prioritised actions behind them, per stakeholder group.
Learn from every move
The question no ranking answers: are your own relationships getting stronger?
Thirteen years ranked first measures standing, not direction. Quarter on quarter, the read shows each relationship's direction of travel — and the instrument compounds: wave by wave it learns what actually shifts each relationship.
HowA quarterly rhythm with listening embedded between waves — every read sharper than the last.
NPS reads one question of one relationship. Brand tracking measures what's said. Employee surveys read one group. Reputation monitoring reads the past. None locates which relationship is weakening, on which driver, or what to do about it. That's the job Trust Intelligence exists to do.
