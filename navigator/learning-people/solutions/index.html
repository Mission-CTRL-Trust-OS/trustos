Activate  Recommended moves

Three moves that turn signal into shared value.

01 · The Verifiable-Outcomes Standard

Make the outcomes claim the most verifiable in the sector.

Learners · Employers · Reviewers
An independently checkable evidence base behind the “97% employment success” claim — methodology published, outcomes verified, refreshed on a schedule — so the strongest number in the story stops being the least proven one.
Validates
Confidence. Whether the promise is matched by published proof.
Unlocks
The “is it legit?” search answered with evidence; an employer pipeline built on verified outcomes.
Learners get outcomes they can verify. Employers get a pipeline they can trust. Learning People wins the search it currently leaves to reviewers. Proven: CIRR (cirr.org) made independently verified job-outcome reporting the shared standard for tech education.
02 · The New-Chapter Baseline

Give the new chapter a stated intent and a measured baseline.

New owner · Team · Learners
A stated direction from the new ownership, paired with a measured trust and culture baseline taken now — so the chapter opens with clarity instead of a vacuum, and every gain from here on is attributable to the choices made in it.
Validates
Clarity. Whether the new chapter is narrated — or left for others to write.
Unlocks
A turnaround story with a start line; a team that hears the direction first.
The team gets a direction it can act on. Learners get a provider that says where it is going. The new owner gets a baseline that makes progress visible. Proven: Satya Nadella’s Hit Refresh (news.microsoft.com/hitrefresh/) turned stated intent into Microsoft’s measured cultural turnaround.
03 · The Echo Repair

Fix the named moments where the learner echo frays.

Sales · Finance · Support
A repair of the four moments the negative signal names — the sales call, the finance agreement, the cancellation policy and mid-course support — measured before and after, so the fix shows up on the review surfaces where every prospect checks.
Validates
Connection. Whether the promise lands the same way at every moment.
Unlocks
A review ecosystem that compounds the fix; fewer grievances reaching the public record.
Learners get a journey that matches the promise. The team gets fewer fires to fight. The review pages start arbitrating in Learning People’s favour. Proven: Octopus Energy (uk.trustpilot.com/review/octopus.energy) turned service moments into a five-star public record at scale.
