Solmax data audit · ten minutes, nothing to prepare

What Solmax already knows

Before we ask anyone at Solmax a single new question, we are working out what the business already holds. This page asks you what exists — not for the files, and not for anything you would have to go and build.

You will see only the lines that touch your work. “We don’t have that”, “not my area” and “I don’t know” are real answers here, and each takes one click.

What TrustOS is

Solmax measures almost everything it does — and almost nothing about how it is experienced. TrustOS fixes that. It asks every group Solmax depends on the same questions, then turns the answers into a single 0–100 score you can track, so you can see where trust is strong and where it is leaking before the numbers move.

Reputation is what people say about what you’ve done. Trust is whether they believe in what you do next.

ClarityWhat’s said — the promises, the messages, the published word.
ConnectionWhat stakeholders actually experience when they deal with Solmax.
ConfidenceWhat can be proved — the evidence behind the claims.

This page is the first step. Before one round of measurement can start, we agree which stakeholder groups to measure and which data sources to read. That means cataloguing what Solmax already has. Nothing here asks you to create anything new.

Dustin Lawrence at MissionCTRL is running this audit for Solmax. Anything unclear, ask him directly at hello@missionctrl.agency.

Step two of five

Which of these do you deal with?

Solmax has five stakeholder relationships in this first wave, each measured on its own. Tick the ones you touch — even loosely. Everything else disappears from your list.

Step three of five

Where do you work?

Some of what we are after is held once, centrally. If you are regional we will mark those lines so you can skip them — unless your region does it differently, which is itself worth knowing.

Step four of five

What exists

Three things per line at most, and only the lines you can speak to. If a line belongs to someone else, name them and we will ask them ourselves.

IntentWhat Solmax set out to be. Strategy, priorities, objectives, targets — as decided, not as published.
ExpectationEverything Solmax says and publishes. Website, datasheets, brand guidelines, decks, brochures, agreements, permits.
RealityWhat people actually experience. Surveys, complaints, quality and safety records, behaviour in the systems.
